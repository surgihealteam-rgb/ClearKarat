Transparency

The Plain Sight Promise

Six things we do in front of you, what is on every slip, and what we will never do.

Six things we do in front of you

An open scale

The weight shows on a display facing you, before and after anything is done to your gold.

Purity tested at your side

You watch the test. We tell you the result and explain it.

An itemised slip

Gross weight, stone or dirt deduction, net weight, purity, rate and charges. One line each.

The quote in writing first

You hold the full figure on paper before any melting or paperwork begins.

Free to say no

Nothing is bought, melted or kept until you agree.

Charges named up front

The loan payoff and any fee or handling charge are listed before you agree.

What is on your slip

LineWhat it means
Gross weightEverything you handed over, as shown on the scale.
Less: stones, lac or dirtWeight that is not gold, removed before pricing.
Net weightGross weight minus the deduction.
PurityThe tested karat, such as 22K or 18K.
Fine goldNet weight multiplied by purity.
RateThe 24K gold rate per gram used, and when it was fixed.
Value at the day's priceFine gold multiplied by the rate.
Less: loan payoff (pledged gold)The exact amount paid to your lender.
Less: any chargeEach fee or handling charge, named.
Money you receiveThe final figure, written and agreed before anything moves.

What we will never do

  • Ask for OTPs, UPI PINs, card numbers or bank passwords.
  • Ask you to send gold or money before you hold a written quote.
  • Add a charge that was not on the slip you agreed to.
  • Pressure you to decide on the spot.
  • Keep or melt your gold before you say yes.

Check us against any other buyer

You are welcome to get quotes elsewhere and compare. Use our calculator to check the arithmetic, and read our guide on choosing a trusted gold buyer. Any buyer who refuses to show you the scale or put the price in writing deserves a second look.
