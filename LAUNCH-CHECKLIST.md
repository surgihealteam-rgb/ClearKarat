# ClearKarat launch checklist

The `website/` folder is the finished site (24 pages, sitemap.xml, robots.txt, social image, schema markup).
Nobody can guarantee the number one spot on Google. This site does the on-page work. The steps below are what move you up.

## 1. Before you publish (30 minutes)
- [ ] Buy your domain (for example clearkarat.com) and host the `website/` folder (Cloudflare Pages, Netlify or GitHub Pages are free).
- [ ] The site assumes the address https://www.clearkarat.com. If yours differs, run: `python3 _source/build.py https://YOURDOMAIN website` (it rewrites every canonical link, the sitemap and the schema).
- [ ] Confirm these items on the site are true for your business, or delete them:
  - Home visit evaluation page (set HOME_VISIT = False in _source/content_core.py to remove it)
  - "Free to say no" and "nothing melted or kept until you agree"
  - Payment is agreed and written on the slip
  - Telugu page wording (ask a native speaker to read it)
- [ ] Add your GSTIN, shop licence or other registration details to the About page and footer once you have them.
- [ ] Add opening hours (they help Google and customers). Tell us and we add them to the page and the schema.

## 2. Google (this is where "near me" rankings come from)
- [ ] Create and verify a Google Business Profile for ClearKarat at Near Manjeera Trinity Homes, Kukatpally, Hyderabad 500072. Category: Gold buyer (or the closest available). Add phone +91 63006 95591, website, hours and photos of the real shop and scale.
- [ ] Add the site to Google Search Console and submit sitemap.xml. Check "Pages" a week later.
- [ ] Ask every happy customer for a Google review (with their permission). Reviews are the strongest local ranking signal. Reply to every review.
- [ ] Never buy fake reviews. Google removes them and can suspend the profile.

## 3. Consistency (name, address, phone)
Use exactly: ClearKarat, Near Manjeera Trinity Homes, Kukatpally, Hyderabad 500072, +91 63006 95591
- [ ] Instagram bio and link: https://www.instagram.com/clearkaratofficial/ (put the website link in the bio).
- [ ] Facebook page, YouTube channel and WhatsApp Business profile with the same details.
- [ ] Listings: Justdial, Sulekha, IndiaMART, Bing Places, Apple Business Connect.

## 4. Keep growing
- [ ] Publish one new guide a month answering a real customer question.
- [ ] Post real photos and short videos of the open scale and the slip (with customer permission).
- [ ] Add real customer reviews and photos to the site once you have written permission.
- [ ] Move to an email address on your own domain (for example care@clearkarat.com) for extra trust.
