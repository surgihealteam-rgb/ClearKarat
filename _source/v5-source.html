The Plain Sight Promise

Six things we do in front of you

Disputes at a gold counter usually come down to weight, purity and charges nobody mentioned. We put those three on the table first.

An open scale

The weight shows on a display facing you, before and after anything is done to your gold.

Purity tested at your side

You watch the test. We tell you the result and how it was measured.

An itemised slip

Gross weight, stone or dirt deduction, net weight, purity, rate and charges. One line each.

The quote in writing first

You hold the full figure on paper before any melting or paperwork begins.

Free to say no

Charges named up front

The loan payoff and any fee or handling charge are listed before you agree, never added at the end.

How it works

From first message to receipt

Each path has five steps. The order matters, because the price is in your hands before the decision is.

Releasing pledged gold

  1. Message us the basicsLender name, loan amount and the approximate weight of the gold.
  2. We confirm the payoffWe check the exact figure with your lender.
  3. You get the written slipThe day's market value of your gold, minus the loan payoff and any charge, equals the money you receive. Every line can be questioned.
  4. You agreeNothing is bought until you say yes.
  5. We clear your loanWe pay the lender the payoff and the pledged gold is released to us.
  6. You receive the restThe remaining money is paid to you, with a receipt for every rupee and every gram.

Selling old gold

  1. Message us a short descriptionWhat you have and roughly how much. Photos help but are not required.
  2. Bring it in or ask howVisit us in person, or message us your city or country and we explain how it works where you are.
  3. Weighed and tested while you watchOpen scale, purity test at your side, result explained.
  4. You receive the itemised slipNet weight, purity, the day's rate and any charge, each on its own line.
  5. Accept or declineAccept and get a payment receipt. Decline and take your gold home.

Questions

      
Straight answers

    

    

      
How is my price worked out?
Net weight, after stones or dirt, times purity, times the day's gold rate, minus any charge listed on your slip. Nothing sits outside those lines.

      
How much money will I receive for pledged gold?
The day's current market value of your gold, minus the loan payoff we clear with your lender and any charge listed on your slip. The rest is paid to you. Each number is on the slip before you agree.

      
What if I do not like the quote?
Say no. Nothing is bought, melted or kept until you agree, and old gold you brought in goes straight back to you. Pledged gold simply stays where it is with your lender.

      
What do I need to release pledged gold?
Photo ID, the lender's name, your loan account number, and the pledge receipt if you have it. If the receipt is lost, tell us. We will explain what your lender can provide instead.

      
What happens to my loan?
We confirm the exact payoff with your lender and clear it. You do not have to arrange the repayment yourself first.

      
Do you work across India and in other countries?
Yes, we serve customers across India and worldwide. Message us your city or country and we will explain exactly how it works for you before you send anything.

      
Is my information safe?
This page does not store what you type. When you press send, your details go into a WhatsApp message on your own phone or computer, and nothing is sent until you tap send there.
