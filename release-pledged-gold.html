How it works, step by step

  1. Message us the basicsLender name, loan amount and the approximate weight of your gold.
  2. We confirm the payoffWe check the exact figure with your lender.
  3. You get the written slipThe day's market value of your gold, minus the loan payoff and any charge, equals the money you receive. Every line can be questioned.
  4. You agreeNothing is bought until you say yes.
  5. We clear your loanWe pay the lender the payoff and the pledged gold is released to us.
  6. You receive the restThe remaining money is paid to you, with a receipt for every rupee and every gram.

What to keep ready

  • Photo ID.
  • The lender's name and your loan account number.
  • The pledge receipt or loan agreement, if you still have it.
  • If the receipt is lost, tell us. We will explain what your lender can provide instead.

What if the gold is worth less than the loan?

If the value of your gold at the day's price is lower than the loan payoff, nothing would be left to pay you. We tell you this on the slip before you decide, and your gold stays with your lender. Nothing changes for you.

Think about timing

Interest on a gold loan keeps building up, and lenders can auction pledged gold if a loan is overdue and notices are ignored. If your loan is close to its due date, tell us when you message so we can help in time. Questions
Straight answers
How do I release pledged gold with ClearKarat?
You tell us the lender, the loan amount and the approximate weight of your gold. We confirm the exact payoff with your lender and give you a written slip. If you agree, we buy your gold at the day's current market price, clear your loan with the lender, and pay you the money that remains.
What if my gold is worth less than my loan?
Then nothing would be left to pay you, and we will tell you so on the slip before you decide. Your pledged gold simply stays where it is with your lender and nothing changes for you.
What documents do I need?
A photo ID is needed for every sale. For pledged gold, also keep your lender's name, your loan account number and the pledge receipt if you still have it. If the receipt is lost, tell us and we will explain what your lender can provide instead. Bills or hallmark papers for old gold help but are not required.
How and when will I be paid?
The amount, the way you are paid and the timing are agreed and written on your slip before anything moves. You will also get a receipt for every rupee paid and every gram handed over.
Do I have to sell my gold after it is weighed and tested?
No. You can say no at any step. Nothing is bought, melted or kept until you agree, and old gold you brought in goes straight back into your hands.
Which areas do you serve?
Our address is near Manjeera Trinity Homes in Kukatpally, Hyderabad, and we serve customers across Hyderabad. We also serve customers elsewhere in India and abroad. Message us your city or country on WhatsApp and we will explain exactly how it works for you before you send anything.
